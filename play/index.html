Every parcel delivered buys you more daylight. Keep the round going until dusk beats you.

Village seed

Built on gama3d · scena3d · anima3d — the world, the people and the rules are all generated in code.

How to play

  • Move — WASD or the arrow keys. On a phone, the stick at the bottom left.
  • Sprint — hold Shift, or the button at the bottom right.
  • Collect — stand on the depot marker in the square to take a parcel.
  • Deliver — the beacon shows the house. Get to its door.
  • The clock — every delivery buys seconds. Streaks buy more.
  • Villagers — walk into one and you both stumble. It costs you time.
